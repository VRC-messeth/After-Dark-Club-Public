# After Dark paintings

Gallery artwork served directly to the After Dark VRChat world. Artist and artwork names are included in each filename. Images are JPEG, at most 2048 pixels on either axis; Witches Flight was resized to meet that limit.

The world uses raw.githubusercontent.com URLs and displays its bundled PLEASE ENABLE UNTRUSTED URLS image until a download succeeds. The manifest records the published dimensions and SHA-256 checksums.
